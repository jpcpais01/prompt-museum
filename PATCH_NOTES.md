# Prompt Museum patch notes

## 2026-10-25: A new icon
- Prompt Museum has a new icon: a gold gallery frame holding a glowing spark, on the museum's night-blue wall.
- It shows up on your Android home screen (including round and themed icons on newer phones) and on the Windows app and taskbar.

## 2026-10-25: Wings come down, screens fill up
- Deleting a wing works again: confirm and the wing comes down, with its prompts kept safe in the museum, unfiled.
- Prompt Museum now opens in full screen. On Windows, press F11 or Alt+Enter to switch between full screen and a window.
- On Android the status and navigation bars tuck away so the museum gets the whole screen; swipe from the edge to bring them back.

## 2026-10-25: A museum for every screen
- Prompt Museum now shapes itself to your screen. On a phone it's the same touch-first app you know; on a computer or a wide window it opens up into a full desktop layout.
- On desktop, a side rail replaces the bottom tab bar, with a New prompt button always in reach.
- Your prompts fill the wall in two or three columns, every wing hangs at once, and the lobby puts your favourites and recent prompts side by side.
- Prompts open with the text on the left and the copy button, tags and notes beside it. The editor gives you a big writing area with the filing details alongside.
- Sheets become centred dialogs on desktop, and settings sit in a tidy grid.
- Keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl+N new prompt, Ctrl+F search, Ctrl+1 to 4 switch tabs, Ctrl+S save while editing, Esc to go back.
- Resize the window and the layout switches live, without losing what you were typing.

## 2026-10-25: Grand opening
- Prompt Museum opens its doors: save your AI prompts, sort them into colourful wings and find any of them in a second.
- A living lobby greets you by name, with your stats, your wings hung as moving paintings, an exhibit of the day, your favourites and what you used last.
- Blanks: write {{topic}} in a prompt and Prompt Museum asks you to fill it in when you copy, with a live preview. Your last answers are remembered.
- Tag prompts with the model they're for (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney and more), add #tags and curator's notes, and star your favourites.
- Live search across titles, text, tags, notes and models, plus filters and sorting in the Library.
- Back up your whole museum by copying it as text, and import it on another device. On Windows you can also save a backup file to Documents.
- Updates from inside the app: when a new version is out you'll see what's new and can download and install it with one tap.
- A small sample exhibition is waiting on first launch so the walls aren't bare.
